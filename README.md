# TOP-RICHEST-BILLIONAIRES-WEALTH-INVESTIGATION
  An SQL-based analysis of the world's 200 richest billionaires, exploring their net worth, age, countries, and industries to uncover wealth distribution patterns and key insights.
## INTRODUCTION 

![](introduction_image.png)


  This project analyses the world's 200 richest individuals using SQL to examine their age, net worth, country, and industry. The aim is to identify patterns in wealth distribution and determine the countries and industries with the highest representation.

## ABOUT THE DATASET

  The Top 200 Richest World's Billionaires Dataset provides a detailed overview of the wealthiest individuals globally, according to the Forbes list. This dataset captures essential information about the top 200 billionaires, offering insights into their financial standing, age, industry, and more. It is a valuable resource for analyzing trends in wealth distribution, industry dominance, and the demographics of the world's richest people.

## COLUMN DESCRIPTION

- **Serial Number:** Sequential numbering of the billionaires in the dataset. • Rank: The global ranking of the billionaire based on their net worth as per the Forbes list. • Name: The name of the billionaire. 
- **Age:** The age of the billionaire. 
- **Country:** The name of the country to which they belong. 
- **Net worth (In Billion Dollars):** The total net worth of the billionaire, expressed in billions of dollars. 
- **Industry:** The industry or sector to which the billionaire's wealth is primarily attributed.

## PROBLEM STATEMENT

  The Global Financial Research Organization wants to understand wealth distribution among the world’s 200 richest people.

## INSIGHTS

- The combined net worth of all 200 billionaires amounts to $6,368 billion approximately ($6.368 trillion).
- Bernard Arnault and family rank first among the 20 richest billionaires based on net worth.
- The five youngest billionaires in the dataset fall within the age range of 31–39 years.
- The total number of billionaires above the age of 50 are 183, i.e. The billionaire population in this dataset is relatively older, suggesting that significant wealth accumulation may be associated with years of business experience and investment.
- The average age of the billionaires is 69.38 years, while their average net worth is $31.84 billion.
- The United States, China, India, Russia, and Germany are the five countries with the highest number of billionaires.
- Technology, Finance & Investments, Fashion & Retail, Food & Beverages, and Diversified are the five industries with the highest number of billionaires.
- Telecom, Technology, Fashion & Retail, Diversified and Automative are the five industries with the highest average net worth.
- The Technology industry has the highest combined net worth among all the industries represented in the dataset.
